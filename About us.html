ABOUT US

Thando's Vehicle Care

Thando's vehicle care was founded in 2024 by now CEO Thando Xaba(22) in Moroka a small township in Soweto.

Welcome to Thando's vehicle care where we focus on providing our customers with the best car washing and detailing services. Our trusted stuff are trained and equiped with skills that separate us from the rest. For best vehicle and motor service visit us at any location near you.

Our Mission

Thando's vehicle care aims to not only wash cars but also give customers the best value for money whilst providing them with the full experience of a clean car with little to no waiting time.
